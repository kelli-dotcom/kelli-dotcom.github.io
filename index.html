My story

Two worlds came together.

“I build for the people doing the work — because I’ve been one of them.”

My path into technology wasn’t a straight line. In 2023, while working as a classroom aide, I started learning tech on the side — inspired by my husband — and earned the Google IT Support Professional Certificate.

The next year, my school launched a lunch program. I stepped into the kitchen and learned the work from the ground up: breakfast and lunch service, food safety, temperature logs, inspections, and all the paperwork that proves the job got done. I became ServSafe certified, kitchen lead, and the certified Person in Charge.

That experience showed me exactly where good people get slowed down by systems that weren’t designed for them. Now I’m putting both sides of my background together: real school experience and growing technical skill.
