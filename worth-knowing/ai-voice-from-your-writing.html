Nobody demos the writing

Every demo of an AI voice tool sounds amazing. Of course it does. They feed it perfect sample text.

Here is what nobody tells you. The voice reads exactly what you give it. A tangled sentence comes out tangled. A run-on comes out breathless. Wrong punctuation and it pauses in the wrong places, like a GPS reading you a grocery list.

Your punctuation is stage directions for the voice. Commas tell it where to breathe. Short sentences sound confident. Clear writing sounds human, and messy writing sounds like a robot, because the robot is just reading your mess out loud.

The reality check. No voice tool fixes bad writing. That is not a flaw in the tool. It is the whole game. Clean the writing first, and the voice takes care of itself.

The workflow, start to finish

Two tools. Both free to try. About fifteen minutes. I am doing this with pages from my book. You can do it with anything you have already written.

  1. Start with words you already have. Mine are book pages. Yours might be a post, a product description, an about page. Read it out loud once, even just in your head. If it sounds stiff on the page, it will sound stiff out loud.
  2. Clean it in Grammarly. Paste it in and take the suggestions that sound like you. Grammarly lives where you write, so there is no copying back and forth. You want clean grammar, short sentences, and punctuation that tells a reader, or a voice, where to pause.
  3. Give it a voice in ElevenLabs. Paste the clean text in, pick a stock voice you like, and press play. That is the whole step on the free tier.
